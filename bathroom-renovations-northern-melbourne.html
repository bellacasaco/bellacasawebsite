LOCAL HOMES

The homes we see in Melbourne's north

Northern Melbourne is a patchwork of eras. Preston and Coburg are known for interwar and post-war brick homes and weatherboards, many with original bathrooms that have been patched and updated over the decades. Essendon has a large number of Federation, Edwardian and interwar homes with generous proportions and plenty of character.

Further out, Doreen and the northern growth corridor are dominated by newer family homes. Their ensuites and main bathrooms are often builder-grade: functional, but flat and ready for something better.

WHAT IS COMMON

What northern homeowners usually ask for

  • ◆Replacing dated tiling and a boxed-in bath in an older brick home with a walk-in shower and a bath that suits the room.
  • ◆Lifting a builder-grade ensuite in a newer home with large-format tile, a custom floating vanity and proper lighting.
  • ◆Family bathrooms that need two people at the vanity and storage that keeps the floor clear.
HOW WE PLAN

What we plan for in older northern homes

  • ◆Surprises behind the walls. Older bathrooms can hide worn waterproofing, uneven floors or past DIY plumbing. If we find something unexpected we stop, show you, and agree the cost impact before continuing.
  • ◆Floor construction. Timber floors and concrete slabs are handled differently when it comes to falls, drainage and floor build-up, so we check this at the site visit.
  • ◆Access and deliveries. Narrow streets and tight driveways affect when tile, vanities and glass can be delivered, and we schedule around that.

FAQ

Questions about renovating a bathroom in Northern Melbourne.

Do you renovate bathrooms in Preston, Coburg and Essendon? +

Yes. Preston, Coburg, Essendon and Doreen are all part of the Northern Melbourne area we service. Tell us your suburb through the contact form and we will arrange a consultation at your home.

Can you renovate a bathroom in an older brick home? +

Yes. Older brick homes are a big part of what we see in the north. We assess the room in person, check the floor and walls, and put the findings into a written scope and a fixed price before any work begins.

Do you work in Doreen and the outer north? +

Yes. Doreen is one of our named service suburbs, and we have renovated a bathroom there. Newer homes in the outer north often suit large-format tile, custom vanities and a proper walk-in shower.
