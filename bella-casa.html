How much does a luxury bathroom or home renovation cost? +

Every home and every renovation is different, so we scope your project in detail before we ever quote a number. What we can promise is a single, fixed price agreed before work begins — no surprise variations once the job is underway.

How long does a typical renovation take? +

Timelines depend on scope, but accelerated scheduling is core to how we work — most bathroom renovations move far faster than the industry-standard multi-month wait. We'll give you a clear, dated schedule as part of your quote.

Is the quote really fixed? +

Yes. We map the full scope of work up front so the price you agree to is the price you pay at handover, with clear communication if you choose to add anything along the way.
