FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does a luxury bathroom renovation cost? +

Bathroom renovations with us typically start from $20,000, with the final price depending on the scope of your project. We assess your project in detail before we ever quote a number, and every project is delivered on a single, fixed price agreed before work begins. If anything changes once work is underway, whether that's something you'd like to add or something unexpected we uncover, we always discuss it and agree on any cost impact with you first.

How long does a typical renovation take? +

We work efficiently to complete every renovation as quickly as the scope safely allows, without cutting corners on quality. The exact timeline depends on the size and complexity of your project, and during your consultation we'll map out a clear, dated schedule so you know when work starts and when it's expected to finish, then manage the project closely to keep it on track.

Is the quote really fixed? +

In the vast majority of cases, yes. We map out the full scope of work up front, so the price you agree to is the price you pay at handover. The only exception is if something unexpected is uncovered once work has started, rare, but possible in any renovation, in which case we always stop and agree on any cost impact with you before continuing.

What areas do you service? +

We service Northern, Eastern, Southern and Western Melbourne. Get in touch and we'll confirm whether your address falls within our current service area.

How do I get started? +

Book a consultation using the form above. We'll discuss your space, your goals and your budget, then follow up with a fixed-price proposal.

Do I need to move out during the renovation? +

Most homeowners stay in the house during a bathroom renovation, though that room is out of use for the length of the job. We'll talk through what's practical for your household as part of the consultation.

What happens if you find something unexpected once work starts? +

Older homes especially can reveal surprises once walls or floors are opened up. If that happens, we stop, show you exactly what we've found and what it means, and agree on next steps and any cost impact together before continuing. You are never billed for extra work without being told first.
