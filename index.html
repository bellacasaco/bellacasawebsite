COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you renovate.

What is included in a Bella Casa Co. bathroom renovation? +

Every fixed-price quote covers the agreed scope from start to finish: design and scoping, every trade the job needs, waterproofing and tiling to Australian Standards, site care and cleanup, project management with one point of contact, and a detailed handover walkthrough. See everything included in our services.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Melbourne? +

Our indicative starting price is $27,500 including GST ($25,000 + GST). That is a base rather than a final quote: the figure can go well above it depending on the size of the room, any layout changes and the finishes you choose. Once we have assessed your space, you receive one fixed price before any work begins. Our bathroom renovation cost guide explains what moves the number up or down.

Can I choose my own tiles and fixtures? +

Yes. Some clients arrive with their tiles, tapware and vanity already chosen; others want guidance. Either way we price the whole job around your selections, and if you would like help we will walk you through options that suit your home and your style.

Which parts of Melbourne do you renovate bathrooms in? +

We work across Northern, Eastern, Southern and Western Melbourne, including Preston, Coburg, Essendon, Doreen, Box Hill, Doncaster, Camberwell, Brighton, St Kilda, Dandenong, Footscray, Werribee and Point Cook. See our service areas page or ask us about your address.

What makes a bathroom renovation 'luxury'? +

It is less about the price tag and more about planning and finish: considered layouts, premium materials such as large-format stone-look or marble-look tile, brushed brass or matte black tapware, frameless glass, custom vanities and layered lighting, all installed over properly waterproofed surfaces. Browse our portfolio to see what that looks like.

Do you renovate ensuites as well as main bathrooms? +

Yes. We renovate ensuites, family and main bathrooms, walk-in showers and wet rooms. Ensuites reward careful planning because every centimetre counts; see our ensuite renovations page for how we approach them.
