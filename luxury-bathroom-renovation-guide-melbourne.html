LUXURY GUIDE

Luxury bathroom renovations in Melbourne, explained.

What makes a bathroom feel luxurious, which materials and layouts are worth the money, and what a luxury renovation costs in Melbourne. Written by a team that builds them on one fixed price.

THE SHORT ANSWER

A luxury bathroom renovation in Melbourne commonly starts in the $30,000s and can pass $50,000 with natural stone, custom joinery or layout changes. Luxury comes from planning and finish, not just the price tag.

THE DETAILS

What makes a bathroom luxury.

A luxury bathroom is usually a handful of well-chosen details done properly, over a layout that suits the room. These are the elements that make the biggest difference.

◆

Materials

Large-format stone-look and marble-look porcelain, or natural stone where it suits the room. Fewer grout lines make surfaces calmer and the room look larger.

◆

Showers and glass

Frameless walk-in screens, ceiling rain heads and step-free wet room floors with linear drains. Clear glass keeps the room feeling open.

◆

Vanities and joinery

Floating, custom or fluted timber vanities with stone or engineered tops. A wall-hung vanity keeps the floor visible and easy to clean.

◆

Lighting

Backlit or LED-lit mirrors, lit niches and layered light that works at six in the morning and ten at night, instead of one harsh downlight.

◆

Fixtures

Brushed brass or matte black tapware, wall-hung toilets and a freestanding bath where there is space. Used sparingly, metal finishes lift a neutral room.

◆

What you cannot see

Waterproofing, falls to the drain, plumbing and the finish of every junction. This is where a luxury result is earned, and where a rushed job fails first.

MATERIALS

Natural marble or marble-look porcelain?

It is the most common luxury question, and neither answer is wrong. Here is how they compare in a wet area.

NATURAL MARBLEMARBLE-LOOK PORCELAIN
LookTruly unique veining, different in every slab.Convincing veining in many styles, with a more consistent finish.
CarePorous. Needs sealing and can stain or etch with some cleaners.Largely non-porous and easy to keep clean, with no sealing.
In wet areasBeautiful, but needs more care over time.Hard-wearing and well suited to showers and floors.
InstallationHeavy slabs and specialist cutting.Large formats need flat surfaces and careful setting.
CostGenerally the higher material and installation cost.Generally lower than natural stone, though premium formats still cost more than standard tile.

Our own projects use marble-look and stone-look porcelain. Gold Marble runs large marble-look tiles with warm gold veining across the walls and floor of an ensuite.

SMALL SPACES

Luxury in a small bathroom or ensuite.

Luxury is not about size. A compact ensuite can feel as considered as a large main bathroom when the layout is planned to the centimetre and the surfaces are calm and consistent. Large tiles, clear glass, a floating vanity and good lighting do most of the work.

See how we approach small rooms on our ensuite renovations page, or browse our bathroom renovation ideas.

FAQ

Luxury bathroom questions, answered.

How much should a luxury bathroom cost? +

Luxury bathroom renovations in Melbourne commonly start in the $30,000s and can pass $50,000 when natural stone, custom joinery, a freestanding bath or major layout changes are involved. What makes a bathroom luxury is the planning, materials and finish rather than the price alone. Our indicative starting price is $27,500 including GST, but a luxury specification usually sits above that base. See our bathroom renovation cost guide for the bands.

Is natural marble worth it in a bathroom? +

It can be, if you love its character and are happy to seal it and care for it. Natural marble is porous, so it can stain or etch, and it costs more to buy and install. Many homeowners get the same look with large marble-look porcelain, which is hard-wearing, needs no sealing and is easier to keep clean. Our Gold Marble ensuite uses marble-look tiles with gold veining.

What is the best tile for a luxury bathroom? +

Large-format porcelain in a stone-look or marble-look finish is a popular choice, because fewer grout lines make a room feel calmer and larger and the surface is durable in wet areas. Natural stone suits owners who want its character and accept the extra care. The best choice depends on the room, the light and how you use the space.

Are backlit mirrors and recessed niches worth it? +

They are two of the most effective details for the money. A backlit or LED-lit mirror gives soft, flattering light at any hour, and a recessed niche keeps bottles out of sight and off the floor. Both must be planned before the walls are lined, so decide early.
