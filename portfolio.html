About these projects

Bathroom renovations in Melbourne, start to finish.

Every project here is a real Melbourne bathroom renovation by Bella Casa Co., shown the way homeowners see it: the room before we started, and the finished result. They range from compact ensuites to generous family bathrooms, and from calm, pale rooms to dark and dramatic ones.

Open any poster to see its drawing sheet, with a before and after slider and numbered callouts for the key materials and fixtures. Each project also has its own page with the starting point, what changed and why the design works.

Thinking about your own bathroom? Read our guide to bathroom renovation costs in Melbourne, see how we approach ensuite renovations, browse bathroom renovation ideas, or check the suburbs we service.

Our indicative starting price is $27,500 including GST, a base that rises with size, layout and finishes. Your fixed price is confirmed after we assess your space. When you are ready, book a consultation.
