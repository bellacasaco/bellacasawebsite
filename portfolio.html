THE GALLERY HALL

Seven real projects.

Seven real Bella Casa Co. bathrooms. Before and after, shown side by side.

MAIN BATHROOM

The Garden Retreat

This bathroom renovation completely changed a dated and mismatched space into a modern bathroom. The old bathroom had dark blue flooring, basic finishes, and an overall heavy feel. We replaced it with large-format, feature 1200x600 tiles, creating a clean, seamless white wall with minimal grout lines. The new frameless glass shower, freestanding bathtub, and updated custom vanity all work together to open up the room. The result is a fresh, modern bathroom with a much lighter, more spacious feel.

ENSUITE

The Marble Ensuite

This bathroom renovation completely transformed an outdated, closed-off space into a modern, luxurious, and more functional design. The old shower enclosure and vanity were replaced with a custom black-framed shower screen, curved timber vanity, and feature backlit mirror to create a more premium feel throughout. Large marble-look tiles paired with brushed brass fixtures added warmth and elegance, while the new lighting and cleaner layout made the bathroom feel brighter and more open. The result is a high-end, modern bathroom that combines practicality with a timeless, luxury finish.

FAMILY BATHROOM

The Double Vanity

This client wanted a bold, contemporary bathroom with high-end finishes and better lighting. We replaced the outdated vanity and tight shower space with a custom floating double vanity, ceiling shower head, ambient LED-lit mirrors, floor-to-ceiling tiles, and a spacious walk-in shower. The darker palette and refined textures turned this bathroom into a modern, hotel-style space.

ENSUITE

The Timber Edit

This client wanted to completely transform their dated, timber bathroom into a modern, timeless space. The vision was clear: brighten the room, maximise functionality, and introduce elegant textures. We removed the heavy wood panelling and replaced it with soft, neutral tiles that instantly opened up and lifted the space. The result is a clean bathroom that feels spacious, luxurious, and built for everyday comfort.

MAIN BATHROOM

The Classic Window

In this project, the client wanted to modernise their outdated bathroom while keeping the original layout. We expanded the shower area and replaced the old tub, tiles, and vanity, bringing in a freestanding bath, floor-to-ceiling stone-look tiles, and a fully glazed shower and bath enclosure. The original arched window stayed in place as a feature, now framed by the new tiling instead of the dated tilework it had before, and a new vanity with brass hardware ties the space together with a warmer, more considered finish. The result is a brighter, more spacious bathroom that still feels true to the home.

ENSUITE

The Curbless Wet Room

This client wanted to get rid of their dated layout and bulky cabinetry, aiming for a more open, minimalist design. We removed the enclosed shower screen, vanity, and toilet layout, replacing it with a seamless, walk-in shower, modern stone-look tiles, and a floating black vanity paired with a vessel basin. The result? A clean, spacious bathroom with warm lighting, elegant fittings, and a timeless finish. A perfect example of how simplicity done right can completely change a space.

FAMILY BATHROOM

The Modern Grey

Family bathrooms take a beating. This one was rebuilt around a modern grey palette and hard-wearing fixtures that look sharp on day one and hold up well past it.
