HOW IT WORKS

Local service, the same standard.

Wherever your home is, the process is the same. We start with a consultation and site assessment in your home, where we measure up, look at the condition of the existing room and talk through what you want from the project, with no obligation and no pressure.

From there you receive a written scope and a single fixed-price quote, a dated schedule, and one point of contact for the life of the project. Waterproofing and tiling are carried out to Australian Standards and checked at every stage, and the site is kept clean and respectful of your home until the final walkthrough.

Bathrooms are our specialty. Our indicative starting price for a bathroom renovation is $27,500 including GST ($25,000 + GST). It is a base that rises with the size of the room, layout and finishes, and your fixed price is confirmed after we assess your space. You can read what shapes the price in our bathroom renovation cost guide. If you are weighing up an ensuite, see our ensuite renovations page.
