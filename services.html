Luxury Bathroom Transformations

From layout to final tile, we manage every stage of your bathroom renovation to a spa-grade standard. Waterproofing and tiling are carried out to Australian Standards, and every fixture is chosen to suit how you actually use the space. This is our core specialty.

  • ◆Design consultation and concept planning
  • ◆Demolition and compliant waterproofing

  • ◆Final detailing and handover walkthrough
